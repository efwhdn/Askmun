Letter From The Secretariat

Distinguished Participants,

Welcome to the first edition of the Model United Nations Conference of Aşık Veysel Fine Arts High School, ASKMUN’26. As the Co-Secretary-Generals, we are very happy and honored to host you. As Ada Eylul Ozdemir and Bulut Acikalin we are proud to be a part of this conference. This conference is not only an organization, it is a result of months of effort, creativity and cooperation. This year we wanted to design the theme of the year for you in a way that suits the artistic spirit of our school, “Art”.Art, since the beginning of human existence, while emotionally feeding humans and nature, also helped them to develop. When we look at the history, we see that art after a while became not only an emotional intensity but also a form of resistance. Even though the reason of this resistance changes from time to time, art has always been the language of someone with brushes, notes or countless ways. In this direction our committees offer you a unique perspective that brings together diplomacy and art.

We have a total of eight committees: Three General Assembly Committees (SOCHUM, Legal, UNWomen) focus on the safety, freedom of expression and privacy of artists. Two special committees (UNFCCC and NATO) deal with global environmental policies and international security issues. And finally three crisis committees (Cabinet of Ivan the Terrible, HCC: Irish Civil War and FCC: League of Legends) push the boundaries between creativity and diplomacy, offering participants a unique discussion environment. While creating these committees, our aim was not only for the delegates to learn diplomatic processes, but also to realize the effect of art on society, history and identity. We, as the ASKMUN’26 family, sincerely believe in the presence of art in every field of life. During this conference what we expect from you is not only to join the debates; but to think, question, create and learn from each other. Each of your perspectives will add something to this conference and us. We hope that ASKMUN’26 will be remembered not only with debates but also with what is learned, with unforgettable memories and happiness. Finally, we sincerely thank the Asik Veysel family, our teachers and everyone who made this conference possible.In this conference where art and diplomacy meet, we are excited to reshape the future of the world with your creativity and vision. Thank you.

Sincerely,

Ada Eylül Özdemir & Bulut Açıkalın

Co-Secretary-Generals
